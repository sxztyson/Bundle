=======================================================
 Map Bundle Collector - Meteor Client Addon
 Made by tysonsxz
=======================================================

Requirements:
- Minecraft: 1.21.11
- Fabric Loader: >= 0.18.2
- Meteor Client for 1.21.11
- Java 21+

Installation Instructions:
1. Make sure you have Fabric Loader and Meteor Client installed for Minecraft 1.21.11.
2. Press Win + R, type `%appdata%\.minecraft\mods` and hit Enter (or navigate to your instance's mods folder).
3. Copy `map-bundle-collector-1.0.0.jar` into your `mods` folder.
4. Launch Minecraft!

Features included:
- Map Bundle Collector: Automatically moves matching filled maps from opened chests/containers into bundles in your inventory.
- Map Art Viewer: Previews multi-part map art directly from bundles in your inventory with custom side panel preview and missing tile detection.

Screenshots:
- Check out the `screenshots/` folder for previews of the inventory side-widget and full-screen map viewer!
